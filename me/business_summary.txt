SunCedar Energy - "Clean power that never sleeps."

SunCedar Energy is a Lebanese solar-energy company founded in 2021 in Beirut by Rania Haddad (Co-founder and CEO) and Karim Aoun (Co-founder and CTO). Our mission is to give every home and small business in Lebanon clean, reliable, around-the-clock electricity, and freedom from noisy, polluting diesel generators. We have installed more than 1,200 systems, employ 38 people, and hold an average customer rating of 4.8 out of 5. The wider team includes Maya Saliba (Head of Customer Success) and Elie Nassar (Lead Installation Engineer).

We design, install and maintain solar and battery systems. Every project starts with a free, no-obligation 60-minute Energy Audit, followed by a written proposal within 48 hours. Homeowners choose from three fixed-price packages: Essential (3 kWp panels, 5 kWh LiFePO4 battery, $3,900), Comfort (5 kWp, 10 kWh, $6,500) and Premium (8 kWp, 15 kWh, $9,800). Prices are in US dollars and include installation, the SunPulse monitoring app and the first year of maintenance. Businesses get custom systems from 10 to 100 kWp, quoted after the audit. We also offer Battery Retrofits for existing solar systems (from $1,800 for 5 kWh) and the CareShield maintenance plan ($15 per month or $150 per year) with two panel cleanings a year, 24/7 remote monitoring and priority repairs within 48 hours. We do not install solar water heaters or EV chargers.

Payment is 30% at signing, 60% on equipment delivery and 10% after commissioning; the Essential and Comfort packages can be paid in 12 interest-free monthly installments through Cedar Community Bank, subject to approval. Warranties: panels 25 years (performance), inverters 10 years, batteries 10 years or 6,000 cycles, workmanship 5 years. A home system typically goes from audit to switch-on in 7 to 14 days.

What makes us different: one accountable team from audit to after-sales, transparent published pricing, LiFePO4-only batteries, live SunPulse monitoring with outage alerts, and a 48-hour repair promise.
